DebugBrief records what you run and what you notice while you debug, then turns it into a brief for your pull request, your handoff, or your incident review. Every line comes from something that actually happened.

No AI. No guessed root causes. No uploads.

pipx install debugbrief

# what it will never do

--- the usual way
+++ debugbrief

  1. -an AI summary of what probably happened
  2. +a record of what actually happened
  3. -"tests pass" because someone said so
  4. +pass or fail from the exit code, nothing else
  5. -a guessed root cause
  6. +Root cause: Not inferred by DebugBrief.
  7. -your session uploaded somewhere
  8. +everything stays in .debugbrief/ on your machine

# evidence explorer

Open a JSON report in your browser to search commands, read output and review changes. It is parsed in this tab only and is never uploaded or stored.

Open the Explorer

# limits

  • No full-screen TUI capture, such as vim or htop.
  • No passive shell-history capture.
  • Redaction is best effort. Review reports before sharing.
  • No native Windows or PowerShell support.
  • No inferred root cause.
