02 / THE PROOF

04 / LIMITS

● the honest part

What it
won’t claim.

DebugBrief is an explicit foreground recorder. It does not watch your machine, call a model, or turn a file diff into a diagnosis.

01No inferred root cause

The report says what was recorded, not what you meant.

02No background watcher

You choose the command. Nothing runs behind your back.

03No silent uploads

Sessions and reports stay under your project’s .debugbrief/ directory.
